## Scope

`index.html`, one-page site for MOMO'S BARBERSHOP (Lloret de Mar). Visitor mode: Persuade, with Operate-grade practicality (open status, phone, WhatsApp, directions). Audience: local men and families plus tourists on their phones. Action: call, WhatsApp, or get directions. Proof: 5.0 on Google from 120 reviews, owner photos of the shop and real cuts, the brothers Moha and Wassim named in reviews. Constraints: no prices, no invented dates or claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shop's own counter poster: a framed bill that answers "are they open, how do I book, where is it" in the first viewport while the clippers run behind it. Refuses the category default of a dark full-bleed photo with a centred "Tu estilo, nuestra pasión" headline and a lone "Reservar" button.

OWN-WORLD: Pinned by the user: the Nonna Lucia gallery poster (framed three-column bill, double rules, slab display, condensed caps labels, stamped statuses, saturated colour fields per section). Translated to Momo's own material: logo black and brass gold, bone paper, barber-pole red and navy as section fields, black marble. Ultra for headings, Rye only inside the logotype lockup, Barlow Condensed caps for labels and numbers, Barlow for body. Double brass rules frame everything; statuses are rubber stamps; section seams are a crawling barber-pole band instead of scallops.

STORY: Visitor sees the shop working (video), knows instantly if it is open today and until when, taps WhatsApp or the phone. Scrolls: the menu of services with the fade guide, real cuts, the two brothers and the shop, what customers say, and a closing bill with the storefront photo and the map.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed looping video (two clips crossfading) under a dark vignette. Inset brass double-rule frame. Desktop three columns: left panel "Horario" with live status headline and the seven days, today highlighted in brass, Sunday stamped Cerrado; centre the real circular logo badge over the video with "Lloret de Mar" ribbon and address; right panel "Reserva" with the mobile number at display size, brass WhatsApp button (primary action), call-the-shop line, address and directions, Google rating. Phones: logo and ribbon over video first, then booking panel, then hours; sticky call/WhatsApp bar after the hero.

FORM: Pinned reference world (Nonna Lucia poster) beats the roll; assigned grounded candidate 5 (clipper-guard fade chart notation) enters as the services section's fade guide, an interactive low/mid/high/skin scale. Kept from declined challengers: nixie counter's single lit accent for live status; kaiju broadcast's stripe crawl as the barber-pole seam. Seed key 5a3fcc89.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction and motion

Hero bill assembles once on load (rules draw, panels settle, logo lands). "Corta por aquí" ticket strip: scissors travel the dashed line with scroll and snip. Barber-pole seams crawl slowly. Fade guide: choosing Bajo/Medio/Alto/Piel slides the transition band along the guard scale. Section content rises in with short staggered reveals; photos unmask with clip-path.
